PCV (Pitch-Count Value) measures the runs a batter produces above the league average per 100 pitches, for the same pitch type, count and location. It splits into two parts:

  • Decision: swinging or taking at the right time;
  • Execution: what the batter gets out of his swings (contact and quality of contact).

Values are shrunk toward the mean according to sample size, which makes batters comparable despite different numbers of pitches seen.
