Count sensitivity

Count sensitivity compares a batter with two strikes and in hitter’s counts, against the same pitch type. It first removes what his overall level predicts: a whiff costs more with two strikes, so a weak hitter falls further behind there without it being specific to the count. A positive value means the batter holds up relatively better with two strikes than ahead in the count; a negative value, that he falls off more than expected.

These differences are small and noisy. Over one season, the most reliable sensitivity this year is decision against fastballs (reliability 0.50); it is the only one charted.
